# Hot Swap: profile research and provenance

Research updated: 1 November 2026. This document distinguishes published player evidence, Blizzard's game-design information, and this addon's own ergonomic decisions. The shipped bindings are a curated starting layout. They are not a measured professional consensus or a demonstrated global optimum.

## What the player research establishes

The strongest readable evidence supports recurring *functions*: direct access to forms, healing without changing the hostile target, separate focus control, and reuse of combat keys when the form changes. It does not establish a universally preferred physical key for each function. Hardware, handedness, movement keys, role, and version affect that choice.

**The requested 20-player comparison is not complete.** A leaderboard lists performance in a particular bracket or activity, not keyboard bindings. For example, [Blizzard's 3v3 leaderboard](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/worldsoul/us/leaderboards/pvp/3v3) is bracket-specific. It should compare action families first and physical-key frequencies only among actually observed maps. This release must not be described as having completed that census.

## Version-specific spell decisions

The Forever data comes from Blizzard's own class deep dives and beta notes. Beta details can change; the live learned spellbook is the final authority for whether an entry is available.

- [Hunter and Druid deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301515/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-hunter-and-druid): supports separate Era/Forever Druid layouts. Forever replaces Tiger's Fury with Shifting Power, adds bear Lacerate and Primal Bite, permits normal Faerie Fire in animal forms, and changes form usability for Barkskin/Nature's Grasp. It also identifies Revive, Wild Growth, Berserk, cat Feral Charge, and Hunter's new active talents. The layout places Forever's new bear attacks on its bear page; Era retains its own toolkit and a separate deliberate powershift control.
- [Mage and Shaman deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302097/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-mage-and-shaman): establishes new Mage attacks and Comprehend Scroll; Shaman receives totem-management actions, Fire Nova, Lava Burst, Riptide, Water Shield, and Rage of the Farseer. Removed Shaman actions are excluded from the Forever catalog. Later [official beta notes](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-26-october/631316/6) additionally identify Frost Blast, which remains distinct from Ice Lance.
- [Priest and Warrior deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301514/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-priest-and-warrior): supports Penance, Prayer of Mending, Binding Heal, Shadow Word: Death, class racial changes, Victory Rush, and Spearing Strike. Divine Grace explicitly cannot target its caster; the catalog marks that exception. This is not an assumption that every new named talent is an active ability.
- [Rogue and Warlock deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock): supports Mutilate/Venom, renamed damaging Banes, Wrack, Incinerate, Bane of Havoc, and removal of Curse of Shadow and Dark Pact. Passive talents such as Demonic Brand are not assigned casting buttons.
- [Paladin deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302099/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-paladin): supports Holy Strike, Seal of Fury, Voice of Truth, Light's Vigil, Swift Judgement, Templar's Bulwark, and Salvation naming changes. Expansion spells such as Divine Storm are not substituted for this toolkit.

## Decisions implemented in the catalog

The Druid profile has four explicit 12-position combat banks: caster, moonkin, cat, and bear. Their keys are 1–5, Shift-1–5, R, and Shift-R. Native Cat paging does not switch when Prowl activates, so Ravage and Pounce stay on Shift-1 and Shift-2. Travel and Aquatic use the caster page. The persistent healing, defensive, dispel, resurrection, supported focus-control, and form controls do not move with the talent build. Repeated healing entries on the caster bank are optional duplicate access paths, not the only way to heal from an animal form.

Form wheel actions deliberately select a form; Era powershifting is a separate Ctrl-R action. Forever uses Shifting Power on Cat R and Berserk on Ctrl-R. The emergency Shift-G action combines Nature's Swiftness with self Healing Touch; standalone Nature's Swiftness stays available on Ctrl-G.

Helpful mouseover actions use explicit Alt-self behavior where the spell permits self targeting. On clients supporting focus, hostile focus variants occupy separate binds, avoiding accidental target switching. Alt-wheel camera zoom is preserved by keeping helpful spells off the unmodified wheel. Trinket slots, item choices, system controls, hardware remapping, and restore behavior are implemented outside this catalog.

All nine class catalogs reserve unlearned talent positions instead of shifting every other bind when a talent changes. Long-reach keys are primarily for travel, conjuring, tracking, and buffs. A learned-spell coverage pass handles uncatalogued active abilities; such fallback placement is coverage, not an assertion of optimal placement.

## All-level catalog audit and Optimize

The Era Druid catalog reserves 54 distinct active spell families across the leveling, trainer, talent, quest-form, and level-60 book-drop toolkit. Bear/Dire Bear count as one upgrade family. Repeated form-page entries, focus variants, the explicit powershift control, and the Nature's Swiftness/heal combination do not inflate that count. Higher ranks retain their family key; separate downrank binds are not created automatically. Talent abilities remain reserved even when the character has a different specialization.

The broader class pass corrected the missing [Distracting Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=20736/distracting-shot) and [Sanctity Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=20218/sanctity-aura) families, and removed passive [Detect Traps](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=2836/detect-traps). Its freed Rogue utility key opens [Poisons](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=2842/poisons); individual crafting recipes are not separate combat abilities. Warlock Healthstone, Soulstone, Spellstone, Firestone, and Detect Invisibility upgrades now select the highest learned family member even when its name changes. IDs were cross-checked against the [original talent-calculator spell dataset](https://git.assemblee-defias.fr/Ravenholdt/Calculateur-Talents-Wow/src/commit/30636c3310e74036aa16b29cb3b61e543cbe16ff/src/data/class-spells.ts), [LibClassicCasterino](https://github.com/Tercioo/Plater-Nameplates/blob/master/libs/LibClassicCasterino/LibClassicCasterino.lua), and the live client's identity checks.

[Petopia's original Classic pet guide](https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/abilities.php) identifies 14 active Hunter pet families, including trainer-taught Growl. All 14 have advance reservations; passive pet armor, stamina, and resistance training do not get cast keys. Warlock pet reservations now cover all four standard demons' active toolkits. First-rank identities were checked against the [original Vanilla pet mappings](https://github.com/cmangos/playerbots/blob/master/playerbot/PlayerbotFactory.cpp); later-expansion ranks from the same source are not imported. Runtime spellbook knowledge remains the casting authority. Pet autocast settings are not changed. Pet types the character does not currently use stay unavailable in the keyboard view.

Blizzard's generated [Spell documentation](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/SpellDocumentation.lua) separates spell metadata from learned status. The [Era SpellBook documentation](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/SpellBookDocumentation.lua) is not evidence that every future trainable spell can be enumerated from the current book. Therefore the catalog reserves future families, while the spellbook adds any learned family that the catalog missed. Unknown spells can display their localized name, icon, and tooltip without being reported as learned. Unverified new Forever IDs remain empty rather than borrowing an expansion's IDs.

Optimize explicitly creates a new named preset from the selected preset's hardware and item preferences, discards that copy's custom binding and fallback assignments, then installs a fresh recommendation with a backup. The original preset stays available. The UI separately reports catalog families, future reservations, and learned spellbook coverage. A successful learned-coverage count alone does not prove every possible future spell was catalogued. Profession recipes, passive talents, racial actions for other races, and unavailable NPC/database-only spells are not additional active class families.

Future combination macros stay inert until every component is verified as learned; an unlearned Nature's Swiftness must not silently turn the emergency combination into an ordinary Healing Touch cast. Learned-ability maintenance updates the addon's unchanged combination macros outside combat. Unknown form placeholders use a no-current-form guard, with runtime form indices repaired once the form is learned. Ordinary spell families retain the same key when higher ranks are learned.

## Native action bars and editable spellbook

Blizzard's [Era ActionBarController](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_ActionBarController/ActionBarController.lua) owns the main button's vehicle, override, temporary form, bonus-form, and manual-page selection. The addon binds those existing buttons rather than creating a second combat bar. Its Druid layout occupies base page 1, Cat 7, Bear 9, and Moonkin 10. Prowl keeps Cat's native page. A key belongs to a position across those pages; replacing Base-1's spell changes Base-1's action while keeping Cat-1's action intact.

The [native MultiActionBars implementation](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_ActionBar/Shared/MultiActionBars.lua) defines extra pages 3–6 and, where present, 13–15. The [matching XML](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_ActionBar/Shared/MultiActionBars.xml) supplies their game-owned frames. Hot Swap detects the available bars, enables those it uses, and assigns overflow actions through ordinary stored-macro keybindings. It snapshots native bar contents before installation and restores macro references by name and verified contents rather than trusting stale macro indices.

The spellbook groups future class families using reviewed [WoWSims spell data](https://github.com/wowsims/gamedata/tree/main/spells), [Classic talent trees](https://github.com/wowsims/classic/tree/master/ui/core/talents/trees), and [Forever talent trees](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/tree/master/ui/sim/talents/trees). These are display classifications, with separate talent markers for each version; the live learned spellbook's skill-line name takes precedence. For example, Era Insect Swarm belongs to Restoration while the Forever classification differs. General and Pets are retained for families that do not belong to a class talent tree.

Show ranks lists learned rank records plus catalog IDs whose identity and rank text can actually be resolved by this client. It does not invent unavailable future rank IDs. The [Classic SpellBookFrame](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_UIPanels_Game/Classic/SpellBookFrame.lua) and generated spell documentation support the separate spellbook and metadata paths. Highest learned rank remains the normal behavior. An explicit rank produces a stored macro with the client's localized rank subtext, so another trained rank does not silently replace it. Additional ranks can occupy separate global keys; a rank replacement on a native form position preserves its page. Missing exact metadata blocks that action instead of falling back to an imported cast name.

Existing user/addon macros are referenced by name and a content fingerprint. They are not copied or recreated, and a missing or changed reference fails preflight. Game and chat commands come from the client's binding-command catalog; raw macro/click commands are excluded from that catalog. Emotes use localized game emote commands. Imported presets carry only typed descriptors, keys, item IDs, and options—never executable Lua or imported macro bodies. Per-preset data bounds protect the synchronous preview/import path; named presets do not have a fixed count limit.

## Client command and focus capability

The reported Era F6 failure exposed an incorrect assumption that a shared command name was supported by every Classic client. Blizzard's [Era Vanilla binding file](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXML/Bindings_Vanilla.xml#L1144) comments out both `FOCUSTARGET` and `TARGETFOCUS`. The [Forever Camelot binding file](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXML/Bindings_Camelot.xml#L1155) actively registers both. Shared [Era slash-command code](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_ChatFrameBase/Shared/SlashCommands.lua#L576) still contains focus handlers, so a function or handler's existence alone does not prove useful client support.

The implementation uses the actual registered command inventory as its capability signal, including commands without keys. Focus is offered only when `TARGETFOCUS` or `FOCUSTARGET` is registered; the F6 target-focus control specifically requires `TARGETFOCUS`. This is independent of the chosen Era/Forever catalog. Unsupported stock focus variants and utilities are omitted, while explicit saved spell/item focus choices produce errors instead of silently targeting something else. An unavailable or incomplete registry cannot establish support, and enumeration is bounded. Fresh application and enabled saved-layout recovery validate native commands before any game mutation. Snapshot restoration continues to preserve preexisting personal macro/addon commands.

Acceptance must include actual Era/Hardcore Optimize with no focus controls, preserved Forever focus support, unsupported imported focus choices producing no writes, and restore of both ordinary personal bindings and saved preset shortcuts. The mock suite exercises these paths but does not certify native focus casting or secure client behavior.

Visible enumeration is not a complete list of valid native commands. The active hidden sections of [Era Vanilla bindings](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/classic_era/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXML/Bindings_Vanilla.xml#L1449) and [Forever Camelot bindings](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/forever/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_FrameXML/Bindings_Camelot.xml#L1517) define `TURNORACTION` and `CAMERAORSELECTORMOVE`, including their button-down and button-up handlers. These two source-backed exceptions stay supported without weakening focus validation or admitting arbitrary command strings. Direct key/action queries supplement snapshots and verification; hidden mouse state is never guessed from a missing visible entry.

Apply now preserves unrelated live bindings and records the command displaced at each planned key. A released owned key restores that prior command only if the player has not changed it since installation. Explicit restore and rollback still recover full snapshots. New snapshots mark that hidden mouse details were captured; older backups preserve current mouse details separately for each binding set. Acceptance includes custom account/character mouse differences, intentional unbinding in new backups, failed setup rollback, and legacy recovery after combat.

## Item choices and targeting evidence

Every item binding records its selected item and manual alternatives. A missing primary is shown as unavailable; an alternative is not substituted automatically. Slot-based trinkets continue to follow slots 13 and 14. Inventory/equipment updates refresh installed item macros from their applied snapshots, preserving any different editing draft.

Unit-target options use explicit verified families, not a broad helpful/harmful-item guess. The original [LibRangeCheck Classic/Forever item lists](https://github.com/WeakAuras/LibRangeCheck-3.0/blob/main/LibRangeCheck-3.0/LibRangeCheck-3.0.lua) identify the ten Classic bandages as friendly unit items and Net-o-Matic, Death Ray, Shrink Ray, and Discombobulator as hostile unit items. The [Classic spell-effect implementation](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/master/src/game/Spells/SpellEffects.cpp) corroborates unit-target handling for Net-o-Matic and Shrink Ray. Bandages can use mouseover, target, self, and client-supported focus behavior; the verified hostile devices cannot self-cast. Ground devices, food, and unverified items retain their item-default targeting. This allowlist is deliberately narrower than every usable item in the game.

## Limits and acceptance work

- New Forever IDs that were not verified are intentionally empty. Exact English learned names can resolve them; baseline spell IDs support localized lookup. Non-English new-spell optimization still needs verified IDs/localization, even when a generic coverage pass can bind the spell.
- The catalog normally selects the highest learned rank. Explicit lower ranks and multiple rank bindings are supported when the client supplies their exact metadata; an exhaustive database of every future rank is not bundled.
- Published macro patterns do not validate secure runtime behavior in the target client. Automated mock tests can validate collisions and state selection; actual combat, form switching, focus support, equipment restrictions, and macro limits still require live-client acceptance testing.
- Ergonomic quality needs use with the player's actual movement habits and mouse. These defaults are configurable recommendations, not a claim that one binding arrangement is optimal for every person or every specialization.
