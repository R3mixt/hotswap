Hot Swap 1.0.3

Add Auto mouseover all spells in Current bindings on clients with native support. Apply changes enables mouseover without a modifier and automatic self-casting. Discard and backups include this setting. Compatible spell buttons use native targeting; macros and addons with separate targeting options keep their own rules.

Fix RestedXP target-macro errors caused by Hot Swap tainting Blizzard's shared slash-command registry. The target-marking fix was confirmed by a local taint trace and the user in Forever after reload.

Improve Current bindings with Bartender4, ElvUI and Dominos: read supported live button and override mappings, preserve original key commands, and reject destinations that change before Apply. Other addons' profiles, paging and overrides remain their own.

Retains 1.0.2's readable action menu and searchable spell replacement. Explicit Mouseover for friendly spells now uses a valid mouseover, then friendly current target, then yourself when the spell permits self-casting. Other targeting modes and item rules are preserved.

Targets Forever 1.60.1 and Classic Era / Hardcore 1.15.9. Action-bar adapters have source review and automated regressions; live addon-specific casting and paging acceptance remains pending. Reload after updating to clear existing taint.

Validation: 583 Lua 5.1 scenarios, 7 packaging checks and 8 publisher checks.
